    How author names are labelled

    As printed in the TOC means the publisher’s TOC. OpenAlex, OpenAlex by DOI, and Crossref by DOI mean names from those databases, used when the TOC had none. Official article page means the journal’s own article page. Items without names show none. Copy a DOI from the item.
