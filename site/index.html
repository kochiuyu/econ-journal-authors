About this release

This is the first public release of the article catalog: the publisher table of contents (TOC) for the core 49 economics journals, 2012–2025. The TOC is the article list. Every item in an issue is kept. Research articles are marked Research article; front matter, editorials, comments, replies, and similar items are marked Other; conference papers are marked Proceedings. The catalog shows research articles first, and the filter shows the rest.

Author names are shown as recorded, with their source. As printed in the TOC means the names come from the publisher’s TOC. OpenAlex (name only) means the TOC had no names and the names come from OpenAlex names recorded by the collector. OpenAlex by DOI (name only) means neither the TOC nor that record had names, so the names come from the OpenAlex work for the item’s DOI. Both are names only, not linked identities. When neither source has names, none are shown. Names are not merged, cleaned, or completed. For TOC bylines stored as “A, B, and C” followed by each name, the printed byline is shown.

Not in this release: a person registry, author identifiers, affiliations, author pages, and any working files from the collection process. Affiliation panels and unique-author pages are planned for later releases. DOIs link to doi.org. When a TOC record has no DOI, the catalog says so.
