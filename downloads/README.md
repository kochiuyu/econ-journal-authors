# Articles TOC catalog, 2012–2025

Public freeze of the Geographic Dispersion core-49 publisher table-of-contents spine.

- Window: 2012–2025 (AER Insights starts in 2019).
- The TOC is the article list. `is_research=1` marks research articles (`article_type=article`); other items are kept and flagged.
- Author names come from the TOC when present, otherwise from OpenAlex names recorded by the collector. This release does **not** include internal `person_id` values, conflict files, or QS handoffs.
- Built 2026-11-11 (Asia/Taipei).

Files: `articles.csv`, `issues.csv`, `journals.csv`, `coverage.csv`, `summary.json`.
